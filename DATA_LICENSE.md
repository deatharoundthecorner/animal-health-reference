# Data license

The **original text and data** in this project (the descriptions, summaries, notes and the way records are selected, structured and linked in `database/seed.sql` and in records added by contributors) are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

You may copy, redistribute and adapt the data for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you give appropriate credit, link to the license and indicate if you made changes.

Suggested attribution:

> Animal Health & Anatomy Reference contributors, CC BY 4.0.

## What this license does not cover

- **Identifiers and terms from public registries** (NCBI Taxonomy IDs, Uberon IDs, Disease Ontology IDs) remain under the terms of the registries that publish them.
- **Third-party sources** that records link to or cite (for example WOAH, WHO, the Merck Veterinary Manual, WSAVA, IUCN) remain under their owners’ copyright. Their text is **not** reproduced in this project, which is why the `sources` table records a license for each source and marks restricted ones “link only”.
- **Images** uploaded by administrators keep whatever license their authors gave them. Only upload images you have the right to publish.

## Important

The sample data is **not expert-reviewed** and is provided without warranty. See `README.md` (“Data and trust”).

If you are a maintainer, confirm that CC BY 4.0 is the license you want for the data before the first public release, and check that the copyright holder named in `LICENSE` is correct.
